FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I test VIDOTEACH?

Contact us and we will provide you or your educational institution with a fully-fledged, free test instance.

Who is VIDOTEACH suitable for?

Teachers, professors, lecturers, trainee teachers and researchers – VIDOTEACH is suitable for anyone who wants to give or receive feedback on presentations or who wants to teach with or analyse videos and PDFs.

What hardware and software do learners and teachers need?

For live feedback, learners only need a device with an internet connection. This can be a smartphone, a tablet or a PC/laptop. Teachers need a device with a stable internet connection and a webcam. To edit videos, users need a PC or laptop and any browser. No other software is required.

What is the minimum age for learners?

We recommend VIDOTEACH from year 5. Learners need to be able to read and write.

How does a cooperation work?

Cooperations can be arranged individually. If you are interested, please contact us.

How can I use VIDOTEACH as a teacher?

There are several ways, for example integrated into your learning platform or as a separate instance. Contact our team and we will find the best solution together.

How can I use VIDOTEACH as a professor?

For example integrated into your learning platform or as a separate instance, including Shibboleth university login. VIDOTEACH can be provided for a single subject area or the entire university. Contact our team and we will find the best solution together.

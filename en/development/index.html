Development

Cooperations, development and history

With the VIDOTEACH application, EGroupware gains an extension for video-supported learning and teaching. Further development and integration into EGroupware is currently carried out in a collaboration between RPTU and EGroupware. The aim is to create a scientifically sound learning platform in EGroupware.

Didactic development of VIDOTEACH

History

History & naming

The roots of VIDOTEACH go back to the “ViKoLe” project at the Rhineland-Palatinate Technical University (RPTU), where the first building block of the tool was developed as a small technical solution. EGroupware GmbH joining the project opened up new options and led to independent research and development.

During a temporary, project-related development cooperation with the University of Tübingen, the tool was known for a while as “LivefeedbackPlus”.

Research and development then continued under the project name “smallPART”. Finally, the interactive video tool received its final name, VIDOTEACH. At the same time, the option of giving teachers live feedback on their presentation was successfully integrated into the solution.

These developments reflect the continuous adaptation and improvement of our tool to the needs of the educational community. The result is a learning video tool based on current scientific findings in didactics.

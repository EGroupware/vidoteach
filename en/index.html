Interactive video tool

With VIDOTEACH, you can provide interactive learning videos, collectively annotate videos and PDFs or receive live feedback on your presentations. Digital, versatile and secure.

Use cases

Wide range of applications

University, traineeship, school, training

Live feedback for presentations

  • Film events and allow feedback to be added to the second
  • Define feedback categories yourself
  • Receive rating clicks, e.g. from a mobile phone, including a time stamp
  • Allow the feedback to be reviewed after the event
Prepare once – use again and again

Video & PDF with learning template

  • Show comments (e.g. instructions) or image markers at the exact time in the video
  • Use comments as jump points
  • Explain processes clearly step by step
  • Make learning with video easier
Ask and answer questions in the video or PDF

Test mode and evaluation

  • Ask questions at the exact time in the video or at a position in the PDF
  • Include single/multiple choice and marking tasks with auto-correction, or set open tasks
  • Keep full control of the exam situation via the settings
  • Export results for documentation with one click

Sources

Interactive learning videos from many sources

Easily use and add didactic value to videos from YouTube, Planet Schule or Panopto, for example. Use your own videos and live feedback recordings or simply integrate other sources.

School & university

Perfect for school & university

Use existing structures

Simple connection to existing systems via standard protocols and SSO (single sign-on). If desired, learners only use their familiar environment and log on to just one platform.

  • Learning platforms such as Moodle, OpenOlat, ILIAS etc. via the LTI interface
  • School account

Learning – but safely

  • Made in Germany: software developed in Germany
  • When used in the cloud, all servers are located in Germany
  • Open source: accessible code ensures transparency
  • Public & non-public contributions
  • Option of anonymous use
  • Visibility control for comments
  • Videos secured with watermarks

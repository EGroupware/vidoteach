Privacy Policy

Information on the processing of personal data pursuant to Art. 13 GDPR. Last updated: November 2026.

1. Summary

This website does not use analytics or tracking tools, or advertising services. It does not load fonts, scripts or videos from third-party servers. It does not set any cookies. We only process personal data that you provide to us yourself – for example via the contact form or by email – and in the technically required server logs.

3. Hosting and server logs

This website is operated by IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Straße 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany. We have concluded a data processing agreement with IONOS pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR.

Each time a page is accessed, your browser automatically transmits data that is stored in server logs:

  • IP address
  • date and time of the request
  • page or file requested
  • referrer URL (the previously visited page)
  • browser type and version, operating system

We need this data to deliver the website, keep it secure and detect errors or attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest pursuant to Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. The logs are not combined with other data or used to analyse user behaviour. They are deleted as soon as they are no longer required for these purposes.

Data is transmitted in encrypted form via HTTPS (TLS).

4. Storage in your browser

Light and dark mode

If you switch between light and dark mode using the icon in the header, we store this choice in your browser's local storage (entries vidoteach-theme and vidoteach-theme-sys). The entries only contain the selected mode, are not transmitted to us and remain stored until you delete them in your browser.

Language selection

If you switch the language using DE/EN at the top, we store this choice in your browser's local storage (entry vidoteach-lang) so that the home page opens in your language on your next visit. The entry is not transmitted to us and remains stored until you delete it in your browser.

Since you explicitly choose these functions yourself, no consent is required (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). No cookies are set for this.

5. Contact by email, phone or fax

When you contact us, we process the information you provide (e.g. name, contact details and your request) in order to handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your request relates to a contract or its initiation, otherwise our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). For the storage period, see section 6.

6. Contact form

Via the contact form we process your first and last name, email address, company, phone number, country and your message. Mandatory fields are marked; without them we cannot handle your request. A hidden field is used only to block spam and contains no personal data.

The form is transmitted to our server in encrypted form and forwarded from there by email to our team, which handles your request in our own EGroupware system. Your data is not passed on to third parties. We use the information to answer your request, advise you and, if you wish, make you an offer. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR), which you give in the form and can withdraw at any time with effect for the future, and Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR for requests relating to a contract.

We generally store data on requests for one year. If no contract is concluded during this time, we delete it afterwards. If a contract is concluded, we store the data for the duration of the contract and beyond that only where statutory retention obligations apply. If you would like us to delete your data earlier, a short message to info@egroupware.org is sufficient.

7. Links to other providers

This website links to services of other providers, for example to www.egroupware.org or to pages of our research partners. These are simple links: only when you click a link does your browser connect to the respective provider, who is then responsible for any further processing. Their privacy policy applies.

11. Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • withdrawal of consent with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3) GDPR)

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): Where we process your data on the basis of our legitimate interest (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR), you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then no longer process the data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds or the processing serves legal claims. You may object to processing for direct marketing purposes at any time without giving reasons.

Simply contact info@egroupware.org or the address above. You can reach our data protection officer directly at datenschutz@egroupware.org.

Right to lodge a complaint: You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Rhineland-Palatinate, Hintere Bleiche 34, 55116 Mainz, Germany, www.datenschutz.rlp.de.

No automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place.
